BEDROOM VORTEX - STUDENT PROMPT WORKBOOK

These prompts walk through the scene-building workflow and design decisions. A single prompt cannot reproduce the supplied scene exactly: generation is variable, and cleanup, lighting, animation, and compositing require iteration.

Use the prompts in numerical order. Save a version after each stage. Start with one prop and a simple gray room, then build complexity. A tool that can only generate an image cannot build an editable Blender scene; use a Blender-capable assistant or implement the instructions manually.

Inputs you supply: your own original artwork or the included generated prop studies; desired room dimensions; output folder; available software.

Suggested checkpoints:
1. One usable prop at real scale.
2. Gray room with correct layout and camera.
3. Day/night lighting stills.
4. Wide vortex test with motion but no finished lighting.
5. Individual shot animation previews.
6. Final rendering and optional Fusion refraction.

Keep the Blender shots at their supplied 24 fps while studying them. They have different source frame ranges and may overlap: they are shot setups, not one contiguous edit timeline. To show every frame in a 25 fps edit, interpret 24 fps footage as 25 fps, producing a 4.1667% speed increase and a duration of 24/25 of the original. Do not also apply another speed increase. If exact duration matters, make a deliberate retiming/interpolation choice or render animation evaluated at 25 fps.
