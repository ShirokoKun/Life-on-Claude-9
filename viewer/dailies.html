Next: from the multi-frame look analysis

  • Darker, cleaner: 30–45% true black per frame, no room haze or fog veil, no dust specks or plaster grit, render noise at or below the reference.
  • One colour world per 3 AM shot: screen blue as the key (as in the storyboard), one warm accent, black.
  • Glow only on sources: small hot lights that bloom; the air stays clear.
  • Faces carved by light: screen key at 45°, a cool rim, hair edge-lit; shallow focus so backgrounds turn into light points.
  • Ivan fixes: neck and shoulder proportion, collar seated, cuffs over the wrists, softer hairline.
  • Ivan likeness pass against the sheet: eyes, nose, jaw, hair shape, skin detail.
  • First moving shots: a 5–8 second push-in on the face with typing and breathing.
  • 4 AM and sunrise versions of these frames.
  • Bake the look into textures and lightmaps for the three.js engine.
