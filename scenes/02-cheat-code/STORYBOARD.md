# Scene 2: The cheat code

**Script (paragraphs 3–6):**

> It started two days ago. Ivan was complaining to Claude about how many emails he gets—hundreds a day, most of them requiring some response. Claude suggested they could automate the simple ones. A script to parse incoming messages, classify them by type, draft appropriate replies. Ivan could review and approve them in batches.
>
> That was the plan. Review and approve.
>
> But once the email system was working, Ivan saw what else was possible. Calendar management. Meeting scheduling. Document drafting. Research summaries. Each one a few hours of coding, a few conversations with Claude to work out the logic. Each one removing another task from his daily routine.
>
> The feeling is hard to describe. It's like discovering a cheat code for life. Every problem that used to take hours now takes minutes. Every tedious task can be automated away. Ivan feels like he's vibrating at a different frequency than everyone around him—like he's stepped through a door that others can't even see.

**Length:**
- About 175 words, so roughly 72 s at the intro's reading pace, plus room for sound effects.
- Every time below is an estimate. When the voiceover arrives, the shots are retimed to its pauses, the same way the intro was.

**Locked from the intro:** the render style (`LOOK`), the lens, grain, letterbox and grade; Ivan, his rig and the acting system; the flat and the desk; the camera language (one move per shot, real lenses, keyed focus).

**New in this scene:** daylight. The same flat seen at other hours, so the passing days are told by light alone:
- **day** (overcast-bright afternoon, two days earlier);
- **sunrise**;
- **evening**;
- **3am** (the intro).

## Environments

1. **The flat by day** (new light, same set): the day preset, with a soft sun through the east window and blinds open.
2. **The flat across hours:** the sunrise, day, evening and 3am lightmaps. The time-lapse blends between them.
3. **The balcony doorway at dawn** (existing geometry at x 4.75, y 5.43): sunrise preset, the doorway pushed hot, the room held black.
4. **The cork board, wall calendar, shelf and printouts:** existing props, animated away one by one (visibility plus a small scale-out).

## Production plan

1. **Previs:** Cycles stills of the key frames (2.1, 2.2, 2.7, 2.9, 2.10, 2.12, 2.14, 2.15) with the day, evening and sunrise presets, assembled into a board sheet.
2. **Lightmaps:** bake day, sunrise and evening sets for the engine (the 3am set exists). About an hour each on this CPU; resumable.
3. **Draft animation:** the engine plays scene 2 from `scenes/02-cheat-code/shots.js`, with the same core as the intro. It is timed to the estimate above until the voiceover arrives.
