Each board is a 4×4 grid, numbered by row (r) and column (c), top-left = r1c1. Captions are the handwritten or typed text above a panel.

**Panel kinds:**
- "Blank" = an empty black panel (board A) or grey panel (boards B, C).
- "Gap fill" = a blank slot that a planned shot occupies in the board's order.

Shot ids refer to `../CHAPTER1_PLAN.md` and `../timeline.json`.
